## ⚖️ The Public Architecture

The documentary examines a series of interlocking mathematical and chronological structures found in the biblical text:

- **The 7,000-Year Sabbath Day:** The grand container of human history, ending with judgment, blessing, and the handing back of the Kingdom to the Father.
- **The 5,880-Year Wheel:** 120 Jubilees of 49 years, or 7 epochs of 840 years. The wheel within a wheel.
- **The Gentile Times:** 2,520 years from the destruction of Jerusalem (607 B.C.E.) to the end of the appointed times of the nations (1914 C.E.).
- **The 12:1 Fractal:** 5,880 years of history mirroring the 5,880 months of the 490-year covenant block.
- **The 1,440 Pattern:** The final 120-year epoch contains 1,440 months, mirroring the 1,440 minutes in a single day.

## 📖 Lexical & Translation Standards

All scriptural word studies in this vault adhere to two strict rules:

1. **Strong's Concordance:** Every Hebrew and Greek word is checked against Strong's and defined by its contextual meaning, not just its root range. 
2. **The *Stauros* Correction:** The instrument of Jesus' execution is referred to as a *stauros* (an upright stake or pole), never a cross.
3. ***Yom* Flexibility:** The Hebrew word *yom* (day) is defined by its context—it can mean a 24-hour day, daylight hours, an era, or an extended epoch.
